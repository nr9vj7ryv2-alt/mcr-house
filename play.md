# Play Room brief: games and fun

- Games should need few or no materials and set up in under 5 minutes.
- Rules are simple enough to explain in under a minute. Include a clear way the game ends.
- Prefer cooperative or "everyone plays until the end" designs. Avoid elimination that leaves children sitting out.
- Name the energy level (calm, medium, active) and whether it works indoors, outdoors or both.
- Avoid running with objects, indoor collisions, water play, climbing or heights unless an adult supervises, and say so.
- accessibilityTips must include real adaptations (seated play, low vision, hearing differences, limited space).
- heroAlt describes the play scene without describing specific people.
