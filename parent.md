# Parent Room brief: tips and support articles

- Tone: a kind friend who has been there. Normalize struggle. Never shame, scare or compare parents.
- Practical first: every article ends with small, doable actions ("Try this today").
- Acknowledge that families differ: single parents, grandparents, foster and adoptive families, different cultures, budgets, and children with different needs.
- No statistics, studies or "experts say". Share ideas as ideas, not as scientific facts.
- No medical, developmental-diagnosis or mental-health advice. If a topic touches health or serious worry, gently suggest talking with a pediatrician, teacher or counselor (use whenToGetHelp).
- Short paragraphs (2 to 3 sentences). Headings that say what the section helps with.
- Avoid absolutes like "always" and "never" and avoid "should".
