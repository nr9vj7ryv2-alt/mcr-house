# Learning Room brief: books and learning activities

- Turn everyday materials and moments into reading, early math, science, language or social-emotional practice.
- Say what children practice in plain words ("counting by twos", "describing what they see"), never promise guaranteed results.
- Discussion questions are open-ended ("What do you think will happen?"), not yes/no.
- Always give a way to make it easier and a way to make it a bit harder.
- Book pairings: only well-known children's books you are certain exist, with the correct author. Leave the list empty if unsure. Never quote or summarize book text.
- Do not copy worksheets or published curriculum. Write original activities.
- Offer alternatives for children who learn differently (hands-on, spoken, visual, movement).
