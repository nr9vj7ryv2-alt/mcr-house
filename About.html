About Monserrat’s Creative Room

Our Mission

At Monserrat’s Creative Room, we help families spend meaningful time together, learn through play, and create. We offer free activities, games, and practical ideas for children, parents, caregivers, and early childhood educators. We plan with neurodiversity and disability inclusion in mind, offering different ways to take part so children with a wide range of abilities can enjoy the experience and shine in their own way.

We humbly seek to be messengers of the Lord by putting kindness, honesty, respect, and compassion into practice. Through stories, traditions, and creative activities from different cultures, we invite families to learn from one another and discover new ways to care for the people around them.

We choose what we share with the same care we would give to choosing something for our own children.

Our Vision

We envision Monserrat’s Creative Room as a trusted online home where families return to play, create, and learn together. We want every child to find something they’re excited to try, a way to take part that works for them, and a chance to feel proud of what they create.

As we grow, we hope to bring families, caregivers, and early childhood educators together across cultures to share ideas, learn from one another, and carry kindness into their homes, classrooms, and communities.

Our Core Values

  • Learning Through Play
  • Creativity
  • Family Connection
  • Kindness & Respect
  • Accessibility & Inclusion
  • Safety & Trust
