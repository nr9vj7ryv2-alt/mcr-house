# Creative Room brief: short art and craft activities

- Use cheap, common household or craft-store materials. Prefer reusing things that would be thrown away.
- 15 to 30 minutes unless the topic needs more. 3 to 8 steps. Each step is one clear action.
- Name quantities and sizes in materials ("8 squares of tissue paper, about 4 inches / 10 cm").
- Say which steps need an adult. Flag choking hazards for children under 3. Avoid hot glue guns and craft knives for young ages unless an adult does that step.
- Mention allergens when food items are used (nuts, wheat, etc.) or suggest non-food swaps.
- Offer a simple variation for children who need more or less support when it fits naturally.
- heroAlt describes the finished craft in one sentence for someone who cannot see it.
- videoNarration is a calm, friendly voice-over, one sentence per step, with no on-screen-only references.
