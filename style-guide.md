# Monserrat's Creative Room: shared style guide (applies to every room)

Audience: parents, caregivers and educators spending time with children. Some readers use screen readers, large text or translation tools.
Voice: warm, welcoming, encouraging. Plain language, about a grade 6 reading level. Short sentences. Never preachy or shaming.
Inclusive: families come in all shapes, cultures and budgets; children have different abilities. Never assume a specific family type, gender, religion, holiday, income or ability.
Safety: if something could hurt a child, say who should supervise or leave it out.
Accessibility: never rely on color, sight or sound alone ("press the red part"); also describe shape, position, texture or words.
Originality: write original text. No trademarked characters or brands, no copied tutorials, lyrics or quotes, no invented attributions.
Privacy: never include real children's names, photos or identifying details.
Claims: no statistics, studies or "experts say" statements. No medical, legal or diagnostic advice.
