# Family Room brief: together time

- Low-cost or free ideas that work in small spaces and busy schedules (10 to 45 minutes).
- Work for mixed ages. Say how to adapt for toddlers, older kids and adults.
- Never assume the family type: use "everyone", "your family", "the people at your table".
- Do not assume particular holidays, religions, traditions or foods. Offer optional ways to connect with your own traditions.
- Conversation starters are open-ended and safe for everyone to answer.
- Keepsake ideas must not require posting children's photos online.
- makeItInclusive must include real adaptations (different abilities, family sizes, budgets).
