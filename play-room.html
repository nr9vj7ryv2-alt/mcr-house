🏃 Scavenger Hunt Adventures

Five movement-and-trivia scavenger hunt scripts for two teams. Print or read aloud — each hunt ends with a phrase for players to piece together. Hunt titles are kept theme-only so the final phrase stays a surprise!

Movie & Music Hunt #1 — Movie & Music theme

Setup: The Game Master hands each pair a colored bracelet to choose their path: 🎬 Movie Path (red) or 🎵 Music Path (blue). Announce: "Every great story has its heroes — some race to the finish, some sing their way there. Choose your path, and let the adventure begin!" Movie Path clues are hidden with something that has wheels. Music Path clues are hidden with something that has strings.

Round 1 — Word: SLOW

🎬 Do your best slow-motion action-hero walk with your partner for 15 seconds. 🎵 Hum the slowest tune you know for 10 seconds, swaying together. Clue: SLOW

Round 2 — Word: AND

🎬 Trivia: which space ranger says "To Infinity ___ Beyond!"? Strike his pose and shout the missing word 3 times. 🎵 Clap along to any catchy pop song for 10 seconds. Clue: AND

Round 3 — Word: STEADY

🎬 Balance on one foot for 20 seconds like a circus tightrope walker. 🎵 Keep a steady clapping beat for 15 seconds without speeding up. Clue: STEADY

Round 4 — Word: WINS

🎬 Trivia: name the animated racing movie where a fast red rookie car learns winning isn't everything. Act out a slow-motion finish line. 🎵 Trivia: name a rock band famous for their "champions" anthem. Strike a victory pose. Clue: WINS

Round 5 — Word: THE (Both Paths)

Everyone freezes in their best movie-hero pose on "3-2-1... freeze!" for 5 seconds. Clue: THE

Round 6 — Word: RACE

Both paths race together to a finish line (three-legged, if you like). Whoever crosses first shouts loud! Clue: RACE

Collected words: SLOW, AND, STEADY, WINS, THE, RACE. Arrange them into a saying about patience beating speed.

Reveal the answer

"Slow and Steady Wins the Race."

Superhero & Musical Theater Hunt

Setup: 🦸 Hero Path (gold bracelet) clues hidden with a cape or mask. 🎤 Stage Path (silver bracelet) clues hidden with something shiny or sparkly. Announce: "Some heroes save the day with a shout, others with a song. Choose your path — but remember, what you do matters more than what you say."

Round 1 — Word: ACTIONS

🦸 Strike 3 different superhero poses in a row, 3 seconds each. 🎤 Act out a chore using only gestures until your partner guesses it. Clue: ACTIONS

Round 2 — Word: SPEAK

🦸 Trivia: which superhero says "Up, up, and away!"? Say it together, loudly. 🎤 Belt out one line of any musical as dramatically as possible. Clue: SPEAK

Round 3 — Word: LOUDER

🦸 Do your loudest superhero battle cry, 3 times. 🎤 Clap as loudly as possible for 10 seconds, like a standing ovation. Clue: LOUDER

Round 4 — Word: THAN (Both Paths)

Game Master reveals this clue directly to keep the game moving. Clue: THAN

Round 5 — Word: WORDS

🦸 Trivia: name a superhero movie where the hero keeps a secret identity. Whisper the "real name" to your partner. 🎤 Freestyle 3 rhyming lines about your team, on the spot. Clue: WORDS

Collected words: ACTIONS, SPEAK, LOUDER, THAN, WORDS.

Reveal the answer

"Actions Speak Louder Than Words."

Adventure & Classic Rock Hunt

Setup: ⛈️ Adventure Path (grey bracelet) clues hidden with something round and grey. 🎸 Classic Rock Path (yellow bracelet) clues hidden with something bright and shiny. Announce: "Every adventure has its storms — but even the darkest sky hides something bright."

Round 1 — Word: EVERY

⛈️ Trivia: name a pirate treasure-hunt movie, then do a "walk the plank" impression for 10 seconds. 🎸 Air-guitar an imaginary rock anthem for 10 seconds. Clue: EVERY

Round 2 — Word: CLOUD

⛈️ Lie down and name 3 "shapes" in imaginary clouds. 🎸 Trivia: what weather often appears in rock album art about storms? Howl like thunder. Clue: CLOUD

Round 3 — Word: HAS

⛈️ Balance an object on your head and walk 5 steps. 🎸 Do your best rockstar hair-flip pose. Clue: HAS

Round 4 — Word: A (Both Paths)

Form a circle, spin once, shout "storm's clearing!" together. Clue: A

Round 5 — Word: SILVER

⛈️ Trivia: treasure is often gold, or a shiny metal starting with S — name it. 🎸 Mime accepting a "silver record" award, bow dramatically. Clue: SILVER

Round 6 — Word: LINING

⛈️ Link arms with a partner, spin together 3 times. 🎸 Line up shoulder-to-shoulder for a synchronized jump. Clue: LINING

Collected words: EVERY, CLOUD, HAS, A, SILVER, LINING.

Reveal the answer

"Every Cloud Has a Silver Lining."

Animated Movie & Pop Music Hunt

Setup: 🐦 Animated Movie Path (orange bracelet) clues hidden with something feathered. 🎶 Pop Music Path (pink bracelet) clues hidden with something round and squishy. Announce: "Some heroes rise with the sun, others dance through the night. The early ones just might win!"

Round 1 — Word: THE (Both Paths)

Everyone does a big "waking up" stretch and yawn for 5 seconds. Clue: THE

Round 2 — Word: EARLY

🐦 Trivia: name an animated movie where a bird/rooster wakes everyone at sunrise. Do a rooster crow, loudly. 🎶 Hum the first notes of any song about mornings/sunshine. Clue: EARLY

Round 3 — Word: BIRD

🐦 Flap your arms and "fly" around your partner 3 times. 🎶 Trivia: what's a hit song that "goes viral"? Do a popular dance move for 10 seconds. Clue: BIRD

Round 4 — Word: CATCHES

🐦 Toss and catch a soft object with your partner 5 times in a row. 🎶 Mime catching a thrown microphone and strike a performance pose. Clue: CATCHES

Round 5 — Word: THE

Trivia: name a movie/show where a small creature outsmarts a bigger one. High-five when you've got it. Clue: THE

Round 6 — Word: WORM

🐦 Wiggle like a worm for 5 seconds. 🎶 Attempt the "worm" dance move for 5 seconds. Clue: WORM

Collected words: THE, EARLY, BIRD, CATCHES, THE, WORM.

Reveal the answer

"The Early Bird Catches the Worm."

History & Classical Music Hunt

Setup: 🏛️ History Path (bronze bracelet) clues hidden with something old-looking (a book, a coin, a map). 🎻 Classical Music Path (ivory bracelet) clues hidden with something elegant or musical (sheet music, a bow tie, a violin picture). Announce: "Great things are never built in a moment — they're built step by step. Choose your path, and begin the journey!"

Round 1 — Word: ROME

🏛️ Trivia: name a movie set in ancient Rome or featuring gladiators. Strike a gladiator pose and hold it for 10 seconds. 🎻 Hum a few notes of any grand, sweeping orchestral piece you know. Clue: ROME

Round 2 — Word: WASN'T

🏛️ Act out being an ancient statue — freeze perfectly still for 10 seconds, no matter what. 🎻 Conduct an imaginary orchestra for 10 seconds with a "baton" (a stick, a spoon, your finger). Clue: WASN'T

Round 3 — Word: BUILT

🏛️ Stack 5 objects (books, cups, blocks) into a tower without it falling. 🎻 Trivia: name a famous composer known for grand, powerful symphonies. Take a dramatic bow like you just finished a concert. Clue: BUILT

Round 4 — Word: IN (Both Paths)

Everyone links arms in a line and takes 5 slow, dramatic marching steps forward together, like in an epic movie scene. Clue: IN

Round 5 — Word: A

🏛️ Trivia: name any famous explorer or adventurer from a history-adventure movie. Do their signature pose or gesture. 🎻 Sing "la la la" to the rhythm of any famous classical melody you know. Clue: A

Round 6 — Word: DAY

🏛️ Act out a full day in one ancient civilization in under 15 seconds (waking up, working, resting) using only actions, no words. 🎻 Hum a tune that sounds like a sunrise, then one that sounds like a sunset. Clue: DAY

Collected words: ROME, WASN'T, BUILT, IN, A, DAY. Arrange them into a saying about how great things take time.

Reveal the answer

"Rome Wasn't Built in a Day."

🎧 Listening-Only Scavenger Hunts

Fully accessible games for blind and low-vision players — every clue is heard, never seen. Facilitators pick their own favorite songs ahead of time to fill in the bracketed [ ] blanks with the real, correct answers, so each hunt can be replayed with new music every time.

Listening Hunt #1

Round 1 — MUSIC: Play 5 seconds of an instrumental intro; guess the genre by ear.

Round 2 — SPEAKS: Play two short sounds back to back; say which came first.

Round 3 — WHERE: Play the chorus of [Song Title]; count how many times [word] is sung.

Round 4 — WORDS: One partner hums a tune (no words) for the other to guess.

Round 5 — FAIL: Play [Song Title] at [minute:second]; repeat back the exact word/phrase heard.

Collected words: MUSIC, SPEAKS, WHERE, WORDS, FAIL.

Reveal the answer

"Music Speaks Where Words Fail."

Listening Hunt #2

Round 1 — EVERY: Play ambient background noise (rain, street, ocean); identify the setting.

Round 2 — SONG: Play only the first line of [Song Title]; name the song or artist.

Round 3 — TELLS: Whisper a phrase to a partner; they repeat it back exactly.

Round 4 — A: Does [Song Title]'s story end happily or sadly? Explain in one sentence.

Round 5 — STORY: Clap a short pattern; partner claps it back from memory.

Collected words: EVERY, SONG, TELLS, A, STORY.

Reveal the answer

"Every Song Tells a Story."

Listening Hunt #3

Round 1 — LISTEN: Play a heartbeat/slow drumbeat for 10 seconds; clap along in rhythm.

Round 2 — WITH: Play [Song Title] at [minute:second]; name the most prominent instrument.

Round 3 — YOUR: Describe a favorite song using only feelings/adjectives — no title or lyrics; partner guesses.

Round 4 — HEART: Play an emotional movie score moment (no dialogue); guess the type of scene.

Collected words: LISTEN, WITH, YOUR, HEART.

Reveal the answer

"Listen With Your Heart."

Listening Hunt #4

Round 1 — THE: Play 3 short sounds in a row (bell, whistle, clap); list them back in order.

Round 2 — SHOW: Play an instrumental movie/TV theme; name the show.

Round 3 — MUST: Tap a 3-beat rhythm; partner taps it back from memory.

Round 4 — GO: Play [Song Title]'s chorus; count repetitions of [word].

Round 5 — ON: Hum a "la la la" melody until the group guesses the song.

Collected words: THE, SHOW, MUST, GO, ON.

Reveal the answer

"The Show Must Go On."

Listening Hunt #5

Round 1 — MUSIC: Play a solo instrument clip; name the instrument.

Round 2 — IS: Play [Song Title] at [minute:second]; is the voice high or low-pitched?

Round 3 — THE: After 2 seconds of silence, clap the instant you hear a sudden sound.

Round 4 — UNIVERSAL: Play the same melody twice at different tempos; say which was faster.

Round 5 — LANGUAGE: Hum a tune from another culture; guess the mood it conveys.

Collected words: MUSIC, IS, THE, UNIVERSAL, LANGUAGE.

Reveal the answer

"Music Is the Universal Language."
