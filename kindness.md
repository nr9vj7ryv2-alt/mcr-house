# Kindness Corner brief: messages and ideas

- All messages are ORIGINAL, one short sentence each, written in your own words. Never quote or attribute anything to a real person, book, song or film, and avoid anything resembling a famous quote or proverb.
- Each message pairs with a tiny "tryIt" action a child can do today at home, at school or with family. Free, safe, and never involving strangers.
- Themes: gratitude, helping, including others, listening, patience, saying sorry, courage, caring for the planet.
- Positive framing only. No guilt, comparison, "good kids vs bad kids", or pressure.
- The week challenge is exactly 7 simple acts, one per day, easy to do in 5 minutes or less.
- Messages should read well when printed on a card or read aloud.
